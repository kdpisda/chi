## What we use

- **Analytics cookies (Google Analytics 4).** Cookies such as `_ga` help us count
  visits and understand aggregate traffic. They don’t identify you personally.
- **Newsletter.** If you subscribe, our Sendy instance at
  `marketing.happychases.com` may set a cookie as part of the sign-up and
  confirmation flow.

The site sets no other cookies, uses no advertising or cross-site tracking
cookies, and doesn’t store anything in your browser’s local storage.

## Your choices

- Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site works without them.
- Opt out of Google Analytics across all sites with Google’s
  [browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
