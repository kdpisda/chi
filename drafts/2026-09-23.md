## Fact check

- "agents have a shell ... In dogfood they did, for benchmarks": shim docstring,
  "Coder agents have a shell ... The dogfood showed exactly this: agents fired
  their own popcorn benchmarks."
- "could burn the rate limit": docstring, "could spam the rate limit or drop the
  user's rank".
- "shim first on their PATH": `agent_env` prepends the shim dir to PATH and pops
  `CHI_GATED_SUBMIT`; wired in `chi/agents/cli_subprocess.py:79-83`.
- "benchmark passes, --mode leaderboard exits 3": exercised the shim locally
  against a fake real CLI. `submit --mode benchmark` forwarded (exit 0);
  `submit --mode leaderboard`, `--mode=leaderboard`, and bare `submit` printed
  "REFUSED by chi" and exited 3. With `CHI_GATED_SUBMIT=1` it forwarded.
- It is a speed bump: the shim only catches the name `popcorn-cli` on PATH, so
  calling the real binary by absolute path bypasses it. Not claimed as a
  boundary.
